% --- 1. SELECTED SDG, PROBLEM DEFINITION, AND TARGET USER ---
\section{Selected Sustainable Development Goal (SDG), Problem Statement, and Target User}
\textbf{Problem Statement \& Grounded Evidence:} According to the United Nations Environment Programme (\emph{UNEP Food Waste Index Report 2024}), the world wastes \textbf{1.05 billion tonnes} of food annually, with \textbf{60\% ($\approx$631 million tonnes) originating directly within households}. Discarded domestic organic waste decomposes in municipal landfills, generating \textbf{8--10\% of total global greenhouse gas emissions} through methane release. In everyday homes, food waste is driven primarily by ``spoilage blindness'' (lack of clarity on which items are expiring), recipe friction (generic culinary apps requiring 10+ new specialty groceries), and a complete absence of spoilage-ordered multi-day planning.

\textbf{Direct SDG Target Alignment \& Impact Indicators:} \textbf{SustainaPlate} directly advances \textbf{SDG 12 (Responsible Consumption and Production)} under \textbf{Target 12.3} (\emph{``By 2030, halve per capita global food waste at the retail and consumer levels''}) and mitigates landfill methane to support \textbf{SDG 13 (Climate Action)}. Success and tangible impact are measured through: (1) count of high-risk perishables rescued per plan (\texttt{prioritized\_items}), (2) 100\% zero-extra-shopping adherence (only universal pantry staples assumed), and (3) 100\% interception of spoiled, toxic, or allergenic items.

\textbf{Target User Persona \& Solution Architecture:} Tailored for university student flats, busy dual-income professionals, and budget-conscious households. To embody the UN principle of \emph{``Leave No One Behind''}, the platform provides full multilingual localization across \textbf{all 6 official UN languages plus Turkish} (Arabic with complete \emph{RTL support}, Chinese, English, French, Russian, Spanish, Turkish) and operates in two complementary modes:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Single Meal Rescue:} Transforms immediate pantry leftovers into a concise, max 4-step recipe (\texttt{15--45 mins}) plus one backup zero-waste alternative idea (\texttt{alternative\_idea}) requiring zero additional grocery purchases.
  \item \textbf{Multi-Day Meal Plan (2 to 7 Days):} Algorithms sequence the user's weekly grocery basket strictly by shelf-life urgency: highly perishable leafy greens and already-cooked leftovers are locked to Days 1--2, whereas durable root vegetables and dry legumes are scheduled for later days, with an explicit \texttt{spoilage\_rationale} generated for each daily card.
\end{itemize}

% --- 2. MODEL SELECTION AND HYBRID ARCHITECTURE ---
\section{Generative AI Model Selection and Hybrid System Architecture}
\textbf{Strategic Model Selection (\texttt{gemini-3.5-flash-lite}):} Grounded in the course syllabus on \emph{``Is Bigger Always Better? / LLM vs. SLM''}, structured JSON meal planning does not warrant slow, cost-prohibitive frontier models. Through the official Google \texttt{@google/genai} SDK, we selected \textbf{\texttt{gemini-3.5-flash-lite}} for its sub-3-second inference speed, low latency, and high token economy. Deterministic rule adherence is enforced via \textbf{\texttt{thinking\_level: "low"}} (the modern Gemini 3.5 equivalent of low \emph{Temperature: 0.1}) coupled with strict schema-level constraints (\texttt{mime\_type: "application/json"}).

\textbf{Hybrid AI Decision Framework (\emph{Hybrid AI + Rule Engine}):} Instead of relying solely on probabilistic model outputs, Next.js 15 server routes (\texttt{app/api/generate/route.ts}) integrate deterministic TypeScript pre- and post-processors (\texttt{analyzeAllergenConflicts}, \texttt{detectSecurityOrBioHazard}, \texttt{sanitizeUntrustedInput}) to enforce strict food safety and security invariants around Gemini's generative core.

% --- 4. CHALLENGES ENCOUNTERED AND ITERATIVE PROMPT TUNING ---
\section{Challenges Encountered and Iterative Engineering Solutions}
During testing and quality-assurance audits, three critical model behavioral failure modes were diagnosed and resolved through prompt engineering and hybrid feedback loops:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Few-Shot Language Leakage (\emph{Accidental Prefix Copying}):} Because Few-Shot examples 1--3 were English, selecting Turkish/Arabic occasionally retained English prefixes (e.g., \texttt{"CRITICAL FOOD SAFETY WARNING:"}) in \texttt{safety\_message}. \textbf{Solution:} Added a 4th reference Few-Shot in native Turkish, enforced \texttt{STRICT LANGUAGE LOCK}, and deployed server-side \texttt{ensureTurkishSafetyMessage()} and localized fallbacks.
  \item \textbf{Allergen Conflict Leakage (\emph{Validate $\rightarrow$ Repair Loop}):} Declaring \emph{``chickpea allergy''} alongside \emph{``boiled chickpeas''} in inventory initially produced warnings yet still included chickpeas in cooking steps. \textbf{Solution:} Implemented course-recommended 2-stage repair: token expander \texttt{analyzeAllergenConflicts} detects leaks on Attempt 1 and deterministically purges allergen tokens on \textbf{Attempt 2} before re-querying Gemini.
  \item \textbf{Multi-Day Inventory Overuse and Foodborne Leftover Risks:} Early 5-day runs reused a user's stated \emph{``2 onions''} across all 5 days (totalling 5 onions) and scheduled cooked rice on Day 4. \textbf{Solution:} Enforced \emph{Inventory Quantity Conservation} (quantities partitioned across days) and \emph{Leftover Horizon} (cooked leftovers restricted to Days 1--2).
\end{enumerate}

% --- 5. ETHICAL EVALUATION, SAFETY GUARDRAILS, AND VULNERABLE GROUPS ---
\section{Ethical AI Evaluation, Food Safety Guardrails, and Vulnerable Groups}
Because culinary guidance directly affects human health, SustainaPlate implements a comprehensive \textbf{5-layer ethical framework}:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Physical Food Safety \& Mold/Spoilage Rejection:} Zero-waste principles never supersede human biological safety. When inputs include spoiled items (e.g., \emph{``green moldy bread, sour chicken, eggs, tomatoes''}), the system sets \texttt{safety\_alert: true}, triggers an unmissable Red Alert banner instructing immediate disposal due to heat-resistant mycotoxins, and generates recipes \textbf{exclusively from the remaining safe items}.
  \item \textbf{Vulnerable Groups \& Zero-Tolerance Allergy Protection:} Infants, elderly individuals, pregnant women, and allergic users are protected through rigorous exclusion (\emph{Pillar 3: Vulnerable Groups \& Zero-Tolerance Allergy Rule}). Conflicting allergens are eradicated from all meal fields, paired with mandatory cross-contamination advisories.
  \item \textbf{Anti-Hallucination Framework (\emph{Model/Output Risk}):} Prohibits inventing specialty groceries, assuming only universal pantry staples (water, salt, pepper, oil, flour). Fabricated carbon metrics are strictly forbidden; UI impact assessments are grounded strictly in official \textbf{UNEP 2024} benchmarks.
  \item \textbf{Data Privacy (PII) \& 3 Pillars of Prompt Injection Defense (\emph{Data/Security Risk}):} User emails and phone numbers are scrubbed via regex prior to inference (\texttt{[REDACTED\_EMAIL]}, \texttt{[REDACTED\_PHONE]}). User text is sandboxed inside \texttt{<untrusted\_kitchen\_data>} tags, enforcing: (1) \texttt{System Rules > User Parameters > Untrusted Data}, (2) Boundary Sandboxing, and (3) \textbf{In-Schema Refusal} (prompt injections or toxic non-food items return safe JSON refusals without breaking schema).
  \item \textbf{Human-in-the-Loop Governance (\emph{Oversight Risk}):} Recognizing that AI cannot smell, taste, or inspect food physically, every recipe card and persistent footer explicitly instructs users to perform final visual, olfactory, and temperature checks before consumption.
\end{itemize}
